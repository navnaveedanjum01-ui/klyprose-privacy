1. Who we are

KlypRose is an Android app that downloads public videos from TikTok, Instagram and Facebook links that you choose, and saves them on your phone. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. "We" and "us" mean the developer of KlypRose.

KlypRose is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by TikTok, Instagram, Facebook or Meta.

2. Information the app handles

Video links and the clipboard

KlypRose reads your clipboard only when you tap Paste & Download. It looks for a video link there and uses it only to download that video. Links you share to KlypRose from other apps are used the same way. The app does not read your clipboard in the background.

Downloaded videos and history

Downloaded videos are saved to the Movies/KlypRose folder on your phone. Your download history (links, video titles, file locations and download status) is stored only on your phone. It is never sent to us or to Google.

Connections to video platforms

To download a video, the app connects directly to the platform the link belongs to (for example TikTok, Instagram or Facebook) and to their video servers, in the same way a web browser would. Those platforms receive the usual technical information any browser sends, such as your IP address and a browser identification string. Their own privacy policies apply to that information.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any account. We do not collect your contacts, photos, location (GPS) or messages, and we do not use analytics or crash-reporting services.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

KlypRose is free and is supported by ads from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC (Google Ireland Limited for users in the EEA and Switzerland). The app shows at most one full-screen ad each time you open it, after you start a download. Whether ads are shown is controlled remotely and they may be switched off at any time.

To show, measure and, where allowed, personalize ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID (and, on newer Android versions, Android's privacy-preserving ad features such as Topics and attribution reporting);
  • your IP address, and an approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information, such as phone model, Android version, language, screen size and app version;
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps.

This information goes directly from the app to Google; we do not receive it. Google handles it under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • How Google uses information for advertising

Depending on your consent and location, Google may work with other ad partners listed in its consent message. A planned KlypRose Premium subscription will remove ads.

4. Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, KlypRose uses Google's consent tool (the User Messaging Platform) to ask for your consent before your data is used for personalized ads. If you do not consent, Google may still show limited or non-personalized ads, which use less data.

You can change your choice at any time in the app: open KlypRose, tap the shield button at the top left to open the Privacy Policy, then tap Ad privacy choices.

5. App settings (Firebase Remote Config)

KlypRose downloads a few settings from Google Firebase Remote Config, such as whether ads are switched on and which ad unit to use. To deliver these settings, Firebase uses a Firebase installation ID (a random identifier for this installation of the app, not linked to you) and basic app and device information. Firebase does not receive your links, videos or download history. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

7. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with anyone for their own purposes. Information is shared with Google only as described in sections 3 to 5, by the services built into the app. Because we do not store your data on any server, we have no personal data of yours to hand over to anyone else.

8. Keeping and deleting data

  • On your phone: your download history stays until you remove entries, use Clear History, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes the history; downloaded videos stay in Movies/KlypRose until you delete them.
  • With Google: Google keeps advertising and Firebase data for the periods described in its policies.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it at any time in Android Settings (usually Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads).

9. Security

Your links, videos and history stay on your phone and are protected by Android's app sandbox. The app's connections to Google services use encrypted HTTPS, and downloads use the connection type of the link you provide, which is HTTPS for TikTok, Instagram and Facebook. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we keep the data the app handles to a minimum.

10. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU and UK GDPR or California privacy law), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent. Where we rely on consent (personalized ads in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), you can withdraw it at any time with Ad privacy choices. Other uses rely on our legitimate interest in providing and funding a free app.

Because we do not hold your data, most requests are best made to Google, for example through My Ad Center or the tools in the Google Privacy Policy. You can also contact us (section 14), and you may complain to your local data protection authority.

11. Children

KlypRose is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app and you have concerns, contact us and we will help.

12. Your responsibility

Only download videos you have the right to save, and respect the creators and each platform's terms of service. KlypRose downloads public content only; it does not bypass logins, private accounts or copy protection.

13. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page and in the app with a new "last updated" date. Please check this page from time to time.

14. Contact

For questions or requests about this policy or your privacy, contact the developer through the contact details on KlypRose's Google Play listing, or open an issue on GitHub (issues there are public, so do not include personal information).
